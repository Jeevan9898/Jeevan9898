01 — About

Building software that creates impact

I'm a Software Engineer passionate about building secure, scalable, and impactful software across Full Stack Development, Blockchain, and Artificial Intelligence. I enjoy solving complex problems and transforming ideas into products that deliver real value. My journey started with curiosity about programming and has grown into a deep interest in software engineering, distributed systems, and intelligent applications.

I have built projects ranging from production-ready MERN applications to Ethereum-based decentralized applications, gaining hands-on experience in frontend development, backend architecture, RESTful APIs, authentication, databases, cloud deployment, and secure smart contract development. On the blockchain side, I've developed DeFi protocols including lending & borrowing platforms, staking systems, decentralized exchanges, DAO governance, NFT marketplaces, and token-based applications using Solidity, Hardhat, and modern Web3 tooling.

Alongside Full Stack and Blockchain development, I'm building expertise in Artificial Intelligence through Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Transformers, and AI-powered applications. I continuously strengthen my foundation in Data Structures & Algorithms, System Design, and Computer Science fundamentals while contributing to open-source projects and exploring emerging technologies.

My goal is to become a world-class Software Engineer capable of building secure, scalable, and impactful products across Web2, Web3, and AI.
